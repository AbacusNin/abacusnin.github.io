Self-assessment

This is a self-reflection prompt, not a skills test. It asks how you would react in the moment, and a self-report like this is a weak stand-in for what you would actually do under pressure. Treat a high score here as a place to focus, not a verdict. The knowledge check is the module that actually tests whether you can recognize a technique and pick the right countermeasure and reporting response.

Everything here runs in your browser. The drawn items and your answers are scored locally and never leave the page. In-progress answers are kept only in this browser's local storage.

Rate each statement from 0 (strongly disagree) to 4 (strongly agree).
